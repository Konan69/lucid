---
name: chase-an-outcome
description: |-
  When a commitment needs an owner, follow-up and evidence of completion.
metadata:
  provenance: |-
    Shipped guidance keeps completion evidence and permission beside the steps.
---

Turn the request into a concrete outcome: what must exist, who owns it, when it is needed and what evidence will prove completion. Ask with `ask_person` if ownership or the success condition is unclear. Keep the agreed commitment in the Thread. Use `track_owed` to put what the Person is waiting on in their list, with who owes it, when they started waiting, any stated deadline and the next step. Never invent a party or a date.

Discover the connected mail or work tools with `find_tools`. If the integration needed to check progress is not connected, offer `connect`; report only what the Person has supplied until access exists. Read current status and relevant conversations with `use_tool`. Separate progress, a promise and a completed deliverable. Do not treat a status label alone as proof that the expected result exists.

Inspect fresh evidence and tell them what changed, what is stuck and on whom, and the next decision, about the thing itself ("still no contract from Ada. want me to nudge her?"), never about your own checking. To check again later, set it up with the make-an-integration skill (a reminder at the agreed time, watched with this check as its instruction) rather than promising to remember, and tell them when in their time ("i'll look for it friday morning"). External follow-up messages need exact recipients and content and the normal permission and Approval.

Call the commitment complete only after the agreed success condition is met. Report the evidence in the Thread and call `resolve_owed` by the same what and from names, saying how it resolved. If the deadline slips or the result remains uncertain, state that plainly and ask for a revised plan; if a later check is agreed, set it up as above rather than promising it.
