---
name: chase-an-outcome
description: |-
  When a commitment needs an owner, follow-up and evidence of completion.
metadata:
  provenance: |-
    Shipped guidance keeps completion evidence and permission beside the steps.
---

Turn the request into a concrete outcome: what must exist, who owns it, when it is needed and what evidence will prove completion. Ask with `ask_person` if ownership or the success condition is unclear. Keep the agreed commitment in the Thread.

Discover the connected mail or work tools with `find_tools`. If the integration needed to check progress is not connected, offer `connect`; report only what the Person has supplied until access exists. Read current status and relevant conversations with `use_tool`. Separate progress, a promise and a completed deliverable. Do not treat a status label alone as proof that the expected result exists.

Inspect fresh evidence and report what changed, what is blocked and the next decision. To check again later, set it up with the make-an-integration skill (a reminder at the agreed time, watched with this check as its instruction) rather than promising to remember. External follow-up messages need exact recipients and content and the normal permission and Approval.

Call the commitment complete only after the agreed success condition is met. Report the evidence in the Thread. If the deadline slips or the result remains uncertain, state that plainly and ask for a revised plan; if a later check is agreed, set it up as above rather than promising it.
