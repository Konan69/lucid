name: remember-and-correct-preferences
description: |-
  When the Person asks Vivid to remember, revise or forget how they like to work.
metadata:
  provenance: |-
    Shipped guidance keeps completion evidence and permission beside the steps.
---

Identify the exact preference or decision the Person wants retained. Keep it useful and self-contained: preferred meeting hours, writing tone or an agreed way to handle work. Do not turn a temporary mood, an inference or a current account status into a permanent fact. Use `ask_person` when the wording or intended audience is unclear.

Use `recall` to inspect related memories before adding another. If the Person corrects an existing preference, use `correct` on the memory shown by its exact words; preserve the new meaning rather than appending a contradictory note. Use `remember` for a new preference only when asked. For a request to remove it, use `forget` and verify its confirmation. Do not claim a correction succeeded after a failed call.

Keep personal preferences private. Share with the Company only when the Person explicitly asks the whole Company to know and has the required permission. State that who may read it before the write. A shared Thread does not by itself grant permission to publish someone's personal preference. Never save passwords, keys, verification codes or account credentials.

Read the returned content and who may read it before saying it was saved, corrected or forgotten. Repeat the substance in plain words so the Person can spot a mistake. A preference can guide future choices but cannot prove live availability or authorize external actions. This procedure needs no integration. If applying the preference later requires an unconnected service, offer that Connection and check current facts there before acting.
